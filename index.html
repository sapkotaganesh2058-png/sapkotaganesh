About

I am a Mechanical Engineer with a growing focus on operations research, currently pursuing an MSc in Renewable Energy Engineering at Pulchowk Campus, Institute of Engineering, Tribhuvan University.

My work spans production scheduling, optimization and mathematical modelling, alongside municipal energy planning, industrial and institutional energy audits, greenhouse-gas projection, carbon-footprint assessment, carbon-finance research and technical reporting.

I am most interested in assignments where analysis has to connect with real operating conditions, policy constraints, economics and implementation decisions.

Areas of practice

  • Operations research. Production scheduling, optimization, scenario and decision analysis, spreadsheet and simulation modelling.
  • Energy systems. Renewable energy assessment, energy planning, demand analysis, scenario modelling and low-carbon technology evaluation.
  • Efficiency and audits. Field measurements, equipment assessment, energy-use diagnostics, conservation measures, savings and payback analysis.
  • Carbon and climate. GHG inventories, emission projections, MRV concepts, carbon pricing, Article 6 and carbon-finance research.
  • Engineering design. 3D modelling, prototyping, technical drawings and technical documentation.

How I work

From field reality to a defensible engineering decision. My strongest work sits between site-level evidence and structured analysis.

Observe and measure

Site visits, equipment inventories, bills, fuel records, surveys, real-time measurements and stakeholder consultation.

Structure the data

Clean datasets, define baselines, identify assumptions and organize the evidence needed for modelling.

Model scenarios

Use spreadsheets, LEAP, SPSS and operations-research methods to compare demand, emissions, costs and interventions.

Translate into action

Turn analysis into technical recommendations, investment logic, policy inputs and implementation priorities.
